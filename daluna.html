The brief

A beautiful product with almost no audience and a storefront that wasn't converting.

DaLuna came to us with strong product-market fit on paper — a loyal pocket of repeat buyers, glowing reviews, and a founder with taste — but the digital footprint didn't match the brand. Instagram was sitting at 6,000 followers with low engagement, the Shopify store was generating around $2,000 a month, and there was no system in place for paid acquisition or influencer marketing.

The goal was simple but ambitious: make DaLuna feel inevitable. Build the audience, rebuild the funnel, and turn the brand into a category contender in one quarter.

What we did

Four moves, one connected system.

We didn't treat social, site, influencers and paid as separate workstreams — they fed each other. Content powered the ads. Influencers powered the social. The new site closed the loop. Every move was measured against revenue, not vanity.

01 · SOCIAL

Instagram, rebuilt from the grid up

A new content system — daily posting, weekly Reels series, a recognisable visual language. We ran the channel end to end: strategy, creative, copy, scheduling, and community.

6K → 25K in 90 days
02 · INFLUENCERS

An influencer programme that actually paid back

We sourced, briefed, gifted and tracked dozens of creator partnerships. Tight briefs, clear UTMs, and a tier structure that let us scale spend on the partners who drove real orders.

Dozens of creators activated
03 · WEBSITE

A Shopify rebuild designed to convert

We redesigned and rebuilt the storefront from scratch — faster, sharper, mobile-first. Product pages structured around objections. Checkout flow stripped of friction. Conversion rate climbed in week one.

Shopify, redesigned & relaunched
04 · PAID MEDIA

Meta & Google ads at 6x ROAS

A creative-led paid strategy — UGC, founder content, and influencer cut-downs feeding cold and warm audiences. Tight measurement, daily iteration, weekly creative refresh.

6x return on ad spend
